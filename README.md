# SleepyDriver; CNN-based Mobile Application for Real-Time Driver Fatigue Detection across Varying Light Conditions 

## About
All over the world, the number of car accidents due to fatigue amounts to 21%. They are destructive and endanger not only their lives but also those of others on the road, especially if they are carrying people between destinations. This is exacerbated at nighttime and in rural roadways, which are often poorly lit. In Sri Lanka itself, bus & tuk-tuk drivers and logistics companies’ truck drivers face a serious risk. Other similar applications require a well-lit environment and rely on eye movement alone to monitor fatigue. User appearance-wise, it is catered to the global population; people of different ethnicities (skin-colours, wearing strong prescription glasses) are underrepresented. 

Therefore, SleepyDriver detects fatigue across <b>varying light environments.</b> From real-time monitoring to an alert system and recommended solutions, this application solves a deadly issue and improves safety by refining a pre-trained AI model.

We follow a Unified Process approach to building this project, saving cost, time, and prioritizing a flexible, incremental, and iterative approach.
